WHY THIS COURSE

Beyond knowing the rules.
Learn how to apply them.

International tax rarely arrives as a textbook question. This course is being developed to help practitioners approach cross-border tax issues with structure, evidence and sound professional judgement.

01

Structured understanding

Separate the OECD framework, relevant South African legislation and selected US rules rather than treating them as interchangeable.

02

Practical applications

Work through documentation questions, intra-group arrangements and technical analyses grounded in practice.

03

Decision-ready thinking

Move from identifying an issue to documenting positions, assumptions, sources and recommendations.

SUBJECT AREAS

A focused view of
international tax practice.

Topics planned for the learning programme. Final published content and availability will be confirmed at launch.

01 / INTERNATIONAL PRESENCE

Permanent establishment & effective management

Explore the concepts that connect a business to taxing jurisdictions and how to frame residency and presence analyses.

02 / INTERCOMPANY DEALINGS

Transfer pricing & documentation

Master File, Local File, country-by-country reporting and documentation considerations for intra-group transactions.

03 / FINANCING

Intra-group loans

Develop a disciplined approach to related-party funding analysis, commercial terms and supportable positions.

04 / CURRENT FRAMEWORKS

Global minimum tax

Understand core concepts, compliance questions and the importance of dated, jurisdiction-specific requirements.

05 / TECHNICAL COMMUNICATION

Technical memos & sources

Organise facts, laws, guidance, uncertainties and conclusions into useful professional advice.
