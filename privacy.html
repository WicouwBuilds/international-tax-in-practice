2. Personal information collected

When you submit a course or corporate training enquiry, we may collect:

  • Your full name and email address.
  • Your buyer classification.
  • Your organisation name, if provided.
  • Your anticipated number of learners.
  • Your areas of course interest.
  • The source and date of your enquiry.
  • Your marketing communication preferences.

For future corporate customers, additional information may include quotations, agreements, invoice details, payment references and learner-access information.

3. Why we collect information

Personal information is collected to:

  • Respond to course enquiries.
  • Provide course and training information.
  • Discuss corporate training requirements.
  • Prepare quotations and licensing arrangements.
  • Maintain accurate business records.
  • Protect the security of our systems.
  • Send marketing communications only where legally permitted.

4. Marketing communications and consent

Requesting information about the course does not automatically constitute consent to receive marketing emails.

Where marketing consent is requested, it is recorded separately from the enquiry. Email verification may be required before the subscription is activated.

Individuals may decline marketing communications without affecting our ability to respond to their enquiries.

Marketing recipients may unsubscribe or object to further direct marketing through the applicable unsubscribe mechanism or by contacting: wicouw@gmail.com .

5. Service providers

We may use third-party technology providers to operate our website, process enquiries and provide educational services.

  • GitHub Pages: Static website hosting.
  • n8n Cloud: Planned enquiry processing, automation and lead management.
  • Brevo: Planned email communications and subscription management.
  • Paystack: Planned direct corporate payment processing.
  • Udemy: Planned individual course distribution and enrollment.

These providers may process information outside South Africa. Applicable international data-transfer arrangements and safeguards will be confirmed before enabling the relevant services.

6. Information security

We aim to implement reasonable organisational and technical measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, loss or misuse.

Access to information will be restricted to authorised individuals and service providers where necessary for legitimate business purposes.

7. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or where retention is otherwise required or permitted by applicable law.

Course enquiries and prospective customers

Personal information relating to unsuccessful or inactive course enquiries is generally retained for a maximum of 12 months after the last meaningful interaction.

A meaningful interaction may include a new enquiry, a substantive response, a discussion about training requirements, or a request for a quotation.

Automated marketing emails, routine system activity and passive website visits do not, by themselves, restart this retention period.

When the retention period expires, information will be reviewed and deleted or irreversibly de-identified unless a lawful reason justifies continued retention.

Customers and corporate agreements

Personal information connected to purchases, contracts, financial transactions and course access may be retained for different periods where necessary to meet contractual, tax, accounting, legal or regulatory obligations.

Marketing preferences

Individuals may unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time.

Limited information necessary to record an unsubscribe request or prevent unwanted marketing may be retained where lawfully justified, without continuing to retain the full enquiry or marketing profile.

Requests for earlier deletion

You may request deletion of personal information before the standard retention period expires, subject to applicable legal and contractual requirements.

Please contact wicouw@gmail.com for privacy-related requests.

8. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable legal requirements, individuals may request:

  • Access to their personal information.
  • Correction of inaccurate information.
  • Deletion of information where appropriate.
  • Objection to certain processing activities.
  • Withdrawal of marketing consent.

Privacy requests may be sent to: wicouw@gmail.com .

Individuals may also contact South Africa's Information Regulator: www.inforegulator.org.za .

9. Cookies and analytics

This initial website does not intentionally deploy advertising or analytics trackers.

This notice will be updated if analytics, embedded forms, additional tracking technologies or third-party media are introduced.

10. Changes to this notice

This privacy notice may be revised to reflect changes in our services, legal obligations or personal-information processing practices.
